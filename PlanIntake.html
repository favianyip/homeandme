PLAN INTAKE & VERIFICATION

The address decides whether the plan is right

A customer can upload the wrong file, a neighbour's plan, or the layout of a flat they used to own. We can't tell from the drawing alone — but the address is on an authoritative register, so it arbitrates. Only plans that agree with their address are modelled, quoted, or kept.

WHERE TO GET THE AUTHENTIC PLAN

HDB flat — buy the official floor plan from HDB's e-service (S$5, owner via SingPass). BTO buyers: it is in your sales brochure.
Condo / EC / landed — owner or MCST buys the approved plans from BCA's Plan Purchasing System (S$35 search + S$45 per set; sellers can be asked to retrieve it).
Any owner — SLA MyProperty holds certified property records free via SingPass.
Portal screenshots and agent sketches are not authentic plans — they are the wrong-scale drawings verification exists to catch.
