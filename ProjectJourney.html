How assisted delivery works

This is not instant or fully-automatic AI rendering. After you upload your floor plan, our team prepares and reviews your labelled 3D model and photorealistic render, then sends the finished result to you. Because a person checks the work, delivery is asynchronous — you upload now and receive your reviewed model and render back later, not on the same screen.

  1. Step 01 Upload your plan Send us an authorised floor plan. It stays a private project input and is never added to a training corpus without your consent.
  2. Step 02 We prepare & review Our team closes walls, hosts every door and window, confirms scale, and prepares a labelled 3D model and photorealistic render — then a person reviews it before anything is sent.
  3. Step 03 Delivered to you We send you the reviewed 3D model and render. It is a concept prepared from your plan — a designer-reviewed scope and formal quote follow separately.

Upload and delivery run only when Project Atelier reports the service enabled below. Until then, this page shows the planned assisted-delivery path and the current service status.
