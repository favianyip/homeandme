BEING STRAIGHT WITH YOU

What these guides will not do.

Same rule as the rest of this site. If we cannot prove it, we will not print it as fact.

NOT A PRICE LIST

No rates, no per-square-foot figures, no cost calculator. Your itemised quote comes from the contractor doing the work, after they see your plan and scope.

NOT PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

Nothing here is structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, regulatory or legal advice. Anything touching safety, approvals or construction goes to a qualified person.

NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR SITE MEASUREMENT

A drawing is evidence of what the drawing shows. Fit, condition and feasibility still need someone measuring the actual flat.

NOT A LAYOUT FOR YOUR FLAT

Flat-type names — 3-room, 4-room, 5-room — do not prove a floor area or an arrangement. Every guide works from the exact plan you actually hold.
