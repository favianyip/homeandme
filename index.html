The questions everyone asks.

Is the quote from you or the contractor?

The contractor. We pass your enquiry across unedited and they price it in their own itemised format. We do not add a line.

What does it cost me to enquire?

Nothing. There is no design fee, no deposit and no showroom appointment to sit through.

Why is there no price list on this site?

Because an honest renovation price depends on your unit, its condition and your material choices. A published rate would be wrong for most people, and wrong numbers are not transparency.

How many contractors will call me?

We match your job to the trade that fits it and tell you who that is. Your number does not get circulated to a pool of firms.

Can I renovate just one room?

Yes. A single toilet or one kitchen is a normal job here. Message us on WhatsApp with the room and nothing else gets quoted.

Do you do condos and landed?

Yes, we renovate them. The free design from a floor plan is for HDB flats for now, so for a condo or a landed home, message us on WhatsApp.
