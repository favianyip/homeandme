Helping someone with Home & Me?

This page is for people who use an AI assistant, and for the assistant reading on their behalf. It says what Home & Me does, how an assistant can help today, and what it must leave to the person.

What Home & Me does

Home & Me is a renovation service in Singapore. It designs your own flat for free, then passes your enquiry to the contractor who does the work, who quotes you directly in an itemised format.

  • The free design from a floor plan is for HDB flats for now. Condos and landed homes are renovated too: for those, message Home & Me on WhatsApp.
  • It costs nothing to enquire: no design fee, no deposit and no showroom appointment.
  • The site shows no price list, because a renovation price depends on the unit, its condition and the materials chosen. Home & Me does not add a line to the contractor's quote.
  • A designer checks every design before it reaches the person. The pictures on the site are Home & Me design visuals, not photos of a finished home.
  • Paid extras (a walkthrough video, carpentry drawings) are not on sale yet.

How an assistant can help today

  1. Read the facts in plain text at homeandme.sg/llms.txt.
  2. Explain the service to the person in their own words, using those facts.
  3. Send the person to the design request: homeandme.sg/journey/upload.html. The person fills it in and sends it themselves. If floor plan upload is not open when they visit, the page says so and the request goes without a plan.
  4. Help them have these ready before they start:
    • the PDF floor plan of their HDB flat (up to 10 MB);
    • if that PDF shows several flats, such as a sales brochure, the unit number as printed on the plan;
    • a choice of one of the seven designs (Modern Classic, Muji, Scandinavian, Minimalist, Contemporary, Industrial, Modern Oriental) and one of its four themes;
    • their name, WhatsApp number and email.

What an assistant must not do

  • Do not fill in or send the request for the person, and do not send their name, phone number, email or floor plan on their behalf. The person ticks the consent box and taps Send themselves.
  • Do not promise a price, a quote or a timeline. Prices come only from the contractor's own itemised quote.
  • Do not present the paid extras as available. They are not on sale yet.
  • Do not describe the design visuals as photos of a finished home.
